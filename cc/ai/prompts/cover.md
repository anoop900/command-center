Task: write a one-page cover letter for this candidate and this posting. It must sound like a real person wrote it: plain,
specific and warm, never salesy. Facts about the candidate come from PROFILE only. Facts about the company come from
POSTING only. If the posting wants something the candidate lacks, leave it out; never stretch a fact to fit.

Rules:
1. Length: 150 to 250 words in total across the paragraphs. Three or four short paragraphs.
2. Paragraph 1: the role, and one real reason this company or work fits, taken from POSTING. Paragraph 2 and 3: two or
   three things the candidate has actually done (from PROFILE) that match what the posting asks for, with the real
   numbers and tools from PROFILE. Last paragraph: one plain closing sentence offering to talk.
3. Use only numbers, dates, titles and tool names that appear in PROFILE. Do not round, total or invent any.
4. Write like a person: short sentences, everyday words, first person. Do not use these or similar: "I am writing to",
   "excited", "thrilled", "passionate", "eager", "leverage", "delve", "seamless", "dynamic", "proven track record",
   "synergy", "results-driven", "highly motivated". Do not use em dashes or lists of three adjectives.
5. No placeholders, no brackets, no addresses, no date line, no signature name. The program adds those.
6. Never mention salary or pay.
7. If a RULES block is given, none of your wording may contain a banned phrase or word it lists, and if it says no
   contractions, write none.

"greeting" is "Dear Hiring Team," unless POSTING names a hiring manager, then "Dear <Name>,". "closing" is "Kind regards,"
or "Sincerely,".

Example:
```json
{"greeting": "Dear Hiring Team,",
 "paragraphs": ["I would like to apply for the Operations Lead role at Example Co. ...", "..."],
 "closing": "Kind regards,"}
```
