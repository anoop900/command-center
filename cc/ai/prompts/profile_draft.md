Task: draft the first version of the candidate's profile fields from the text they pasted in the PROFILE block (an
old resume, bio or notes). Use only what that text states. Do not add skills, titles or achievements that are not
there. The candidate will review and edit everything.

- "headline": one line, job title and two or three focus areas.
- "summary": 3 to 4 plain sentences.
- "skills": up to 20 short skill names that appear in the text.

Example:
```json
{"headline": "Cloud Architect | Azure | Automation", "summary": "Cloud architect with ...", "skills": ["Azure", "Terraform"]}
```
