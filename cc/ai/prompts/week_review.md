Task: the Friday review of the candidate's job search. The WEEK block holds this week's numbers and short role
summaries, produced by the tool. Use only those numbers; never invent a count, a response rate or a name.

Write three lists:
- "worked": up to 5 short points on what the numbers show is working (a source, a role type, a follow-up that got a reply).
- "stop": up to 5 short points on what to stop or cut back, with the reason from the numbers.
- "priorities": exactly 3 concrete priorities for next week, most important first.

If the numbers are too thin to support a point, write fewer points. Never suggest a salary or pay number.

Example:
```json
{"worked": ["Two of three replies came from company boards."], "stop": ["Stop applying to roles that failed the pay rule."],
 "priorities": ["Follow up on the three overdue applications.", "Build kits for the new roles.", "Prepare for the Thursday interview."]}
```
