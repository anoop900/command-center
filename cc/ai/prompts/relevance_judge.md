Task: decide whether one job is worth the job seeker's attention. Code already let it through the hard rules (pay,
place, levels, their own word lists); it is borderline only because some title words resemble roles they rejected
before. Judge the role itself, not the words.

Use the PROFILE (what they have done and want), the DECISIONS (what they did with past titles: "positive" means they
applied or got a response, "not_fit" means they said it was not a match) and the POSTING (title and text). Answer
"yes" when the duties fit the profile's direction and resemble positive roles more than not_fit ones; answer "no" when
the work is a different field or a clearly different job. When the posting text is missing and the title alone does
not settle it, answer "yes" (a person will look). "why" is one short sentence naming the deciding fact.

Example:
```json
{"fit": "no", "why": "A hands-on Java developer role; the profile and positive roles are security architecture."}
```
